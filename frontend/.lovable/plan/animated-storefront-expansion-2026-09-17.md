# Animated Storefront Expansion

## What will change

- Replace the single top banner with a three-slide promotional carousel.
- Auto-advance the banners, while providing dots and previous/next controls for manual navigation.
- Expand the catalog to 30 products and present them in a horizontally sliding, responsive carousel.
- Expand the services area to 15 services in its own responsive carousel.
- Keep the current orange, navy, and bright marketplace visual style.

## Motion and interaction

- Use smooth slide transitions with restrained fades and image movement.
- Pause automatic banner movement when a visitor interacts with it.
- Add polished button, category, product, and service motion without making the page distracting.
- Respect reduced-motion device settings.

## Responsive behavior

- Show fewer carousel items on smaller screens and more on wider screens.
- Keep touch-friendly swiping and accessible navigation controls.
- Prevent titles, prices, badges, and controls from overlapping at narrow widths.

## Verification

- Check automatic and manual banner navigation.
- Check all 30 products and 15 services are reachable.
- Review desktop and mobile layouts for clipping, overlap, and console errors.
